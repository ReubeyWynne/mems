The 28-day clock

Four weeks, one loop. After your kingdom's first Kingdom of Power, the same four weeks repeat forever, and KvK matches on a global 28-day cycle, so every kingdom runs its prep and battle in the same calendar week. Which week you're in is the whole game.

1–7BrawlSwordland Showdown's week: its Sunday battle, one of two in the cycle. No fixed daily prep themes; keep building; Strongest Governor is next.
8–14GovernorStrongest Governor: seven personal days, a different theme each day. Spend on the days that match your hoard.
15–21MobilizeAlliance Mobilization: its own event, no fixed daily themes, and Swordland Showdown's battle runs again on its Sunday. Spend minimal KvK-precious resources; days 20–21 are matchmaking, and day 21 is when you hold intel for prep.
22–28KvKKingdom of Power: five prep days that decide who attacks, then the castle battle weekend.

The wheel is global: one date anchors it for every kingdom. New kingdoms join the wheel once they unlock Kingdom of Power, after their 5th Hall of Governors; before that, their events run on their own clock. The live clock above works today out from the date; correct it in the calibration box if your device date differs from the game's.

The other clocks still run underneath: Bear Hunt every two days, Mystic Trial rooms on their weekdays, Arena daily. They're the income; the 28-day wheel is the spending.

Two things shape every SG plan: milestones scale with your Town Center group (higher groups need more points than lower ones), and the rewards ladder runs daily ranks (at the day rollover), challenge medals, kingdom and cross-kingdom rank rewards, and a kingdom-state reward bar to qualify for kingdom-state rewards when your kingdom finishes near the top of its matchmaking group.

Most of the time, rankings aren't worth chasing here. Strongest Governor's rank rewards are mostly time-limited cosmetics, with little permanent progression. The fixed rewards (daily milestones, challenge medals, the kingdom-state reward bar) are the value. Your kingdom's KvK record is what it's remembered for, and it's what shapes how Transfer treats the kingdom: save the hoard for prep, not for an SG leaderboard.

The battle weekend

Prep decides who attacks; the weekend decides who holds. The prep winner enters the loser's kingdom and tries to take the Castle: a five-hour contest won by one clean 2.5-hour hold, or by holding longest when the clock runs out.

  • The Castle: the contest runs a full 5 hours. Hold it 2.5 hours continuously as one alliance and you win on the spot; otherwise the alliance with the most total hold time when the window closes takes it. The game doesn't end early: if no one lands the continuous hold, the full 5 hours run even once a side is mathematically uncatchable. Hold time is by alliance, not kingdom.
  • The turrets: four of them. They wear down the defenders and give their occupiers lethality in the Castle fight.
  • The window: the battle day runs ~10:00–22:00 UTC, and the Castle is contestable for the full 5 hours within it. Know your active window in UTC.
  • The window itself: during 10:00–22:00 UTC, players can teleport between the servers and troop kills score points, All-Out style. It's a fun place to blow off steam, but don't burn too many troops and resources. Check world and alliance chat for any rules of engagement agreed between the servers before you join in.
  • The ±3 TC rule: your Town Center's attack range gates who you can hit. It decides your rally assignments before the weekend starts.
  • Field Triage: 30% base troop recovery, up to 90% with Medical Satchels and Rescue Orders. Healing burns your stockpiled food and wood; that's what the prep hoard is for.
  • The shield: activate it before the battle window opens. There is always a window between phases where unshielded towns get hit, agreed rules or not, and the shield is essential for any time you spend offline during the battle day.

The routine

The whole page, in the order you'll actually do it: the save list for prep week, then the day-one ritual, then the weekend:

  1. 1

    Save the taming marks

    Advanced and Common Taming Marks; they're the day 3 and day 5 stars.

  2. 2

    Save the gems and shards

    Hero Roulette runs on days 2 and 3; save the gems for it (spins are event-only) and every hero shard tier.

  3. 3

    Save the truegold

    Truegold and Tempered Truegold: days 1, 2 and 5.

  4. 4

    Save the gear materials

    Mithril, widgets and forgehammers: days 4 and 5. Master emblems and manuscripts: days 2 and 3.

  5. 5

    Keep the troops one tier down

    Come into prep week with troops a tier below your max, ready to promote, for more points per speedup spent.

  6. 6

    Stack the buffs before day 1

    Wolf pet buff, Double Time Decree, Chief Minister if the kingdom runs it; activate them before you spend, then spend.

  7. 7

    Hold the intel, empty the hospital

    Stop collecting Watchtower missions from 08:00 the day before a scoring day (on day 21 before prep), so the banked missions cash in for points. Empty the hospital before battle week too; healing eats the hoard.

  8. 8

    Shield before the window

    Activate your town's shield as prep ends. The gap between phases is when unshielded towns fall.

  9. 9

    Know the battle plan

    Your UTC window, your Town Center's attack range, your rally assignments, and if you're top-150 by power, check your Duel of the Elites group before you stop at 200,000. Watch days 20–21 for the matchmaking reveal; know your opponent before prep opens.
