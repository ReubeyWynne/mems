The maths

    
How the ratio is worked out:

    
Each troop type's A factor is its attack times its lethality:
      (1 + attack%) × (1 + lethality%). One factor per type, from the lead's report.

    
The damage weights are infantry ⅓, cavalry 1, archers 4.4/3 — the archers'
      extra ×1.1 because the bear is all infantry. The ideal share is the weight squared, normalised:
      ft ∝ (weightt·At)².

    

      The formula, in one line
      

        
ft ∝ (Ainf/3, Acav, 4.4·Aarc/3)² · K = √(Σ wt²)

      

    

    
The bear factor and hero skills multiply absolute damage but
      cancel out of the ratio — the mix depends only on the lead's attack factors. More sims (heroes, full
      damage, PvP) are being researched; this is the first.
