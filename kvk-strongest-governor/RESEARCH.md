# KvK + Strongest Governor — research for the 2-in-1 page

Working research for the next event page. Sources were fetched live on 2026-09-01 from
the three links the alliance provided plus the supporting sites; every number below is
cross-checked against at least two of them. Anything that is a community guess or a
to-verify is marked `⚠ verify`.

This page is a **2-in-1** because the two events are the same machine: a day-by-day
scoring event where each day's active-task list decides what your hoard is worth.
KvK prep is the 5-day kingdom version; Strongest Governor is the 7-day personal
version. The killer app is a **live "what's efficient today" display** that knows
where you are in the 28-day cycle and hands you a KingShot-ready copy block.

---

## 1 · The 28-day cycle (the master clock)

Kingshot runs four big weeks on a repeating loop after a kingdom's first KvK:

```
Brawl week (Swordland Showdown) → Strongest Governor week → Alliance Mobilization week → KvK Prep week
```

- Every phase runs 7 days; **KvK Prep week = 5 prep-scoring days + the castle-battle weekend**.
- KvK matches on a **global 28-day cycle**: every kingdom's matchmaking, prep, and battle
  happen in the same calendar week, at the same time, worldwide.
- **Strongest Governor** appears **once per 4-week cycle** (monthly); it replaced
  "Hall of Governors" on older kingdoms. Its day order: City Construction → Hero
  Development → Skill Up → Combat Training → Skill Up → Combat Training → Hero Development.
- Newer kingdoms unlock KvK only after their **5th Hall of Governors** event.
- ⚠ Only the Strongest Governor slot is independently verified in-client; the other
  three weeks are a community-reported ordering. **The schedule is GLOBAL — the owner
  confirms (2026-09-02) we are on cycle day 17**, and **day 1 is always a Monday**, so
  the wheel is anchored to real dates: **cycle day 1 = Monday 2026-08-17** (2026-09-01
  is day 16; KvK prep opens on day 22, 2026-09-07; the battle weekend is days 27–28,
  2026-09-12/13). The live display derives today's day from the date; a calibration
  input re-anchors day 1 if a device date ever differs from the game's. Kingdoms that
  haven't yet unlocked KvK (pre-5th-Hall-of-Governors) run their own clock until they
  join the global wheel.
- **Days 20–21 are the KvK matchmaking window** — the opponent reveal happens there,
  before prep opens on day 22. Day 21 is the intel-hold day: stop collecting
  Watchtower missions from 08:00 so they bank and cash in for prep points.
- **Swordland Showdown runs twice a cycle** — the Sunday of Brawl week AND the
  Sunday of Mobilization week (days 7 and 21 under the Monday anchor; the earlier
  day 6 / day 20 numbers came from the old Tuesday-anchored draft) — the user's
  correction over the community calendar, which only credits Brawl week.
- **Alliance Mobilization is its own event**, not a quiet week — it has no fixed
  daily themes. The guidance is minimal spend: skip the Armament Competition /
  Officer Project rankings, take the fixed rewards with minimal resources, and keep the
  hoard for prep.

Sources: [kingshotmastery event calendar](https://kingshotmastery.com/guides/event-calendar),
[kingshotoptimizer KvK guide](https://kingshotoptimizer.com/kvk-rankings/guide/).

### What runs when (Kingshot's other clocks, for the page's context)

- Bear Hunt: every 2 days, 30 min — Forgehammers + gear materials (never skip).
- Mystic Trial: daily rotating rooms (Mon–Tue heroes, Wed–Thu pets, Fri–Sat research + gov gear, Sun full check).
- Arena: daily, 5 attempts.
- Swordland Showdown: Brawl week, Sunday, 1-hour battle (already on dey.ci).
- Tri-Alliance Clash: every 4 weeks, Saturday.

## 2 · Kingdom of Power (KvK) — structure

- **What it is:** the flagship PvP event. Two kingdoms, a **5-day prep phase** (scoring)
  then a **battle phase** where the prep winner invades the loser's kingdom and tries to
  capture the Castle.
- **Matchmaking:** kingdom must be **70+ days old**; matched by kingdom power, similar
  range; an unmatched kingdom gets a **Bye** (free round, fewer rewards).
- **Two phases scored separately:** a kingdom can win prep and lose battle (ratings for
  the two phases are only ~31% correlated).
- **Prep wins the attacking position** + State Bonus buffs for the battle weekend.

### KvK prep day themes

| Day | Theme | What scores |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **City Construction** | building upgrades + Truegold family, construction/research/training/learning speedups, intel missions, governor charm score |
| 2 | **Basic Skills Up** | hero roulette, hero shard ascensions, master emblems/manuscripts, research, speedups, gathering |
| 3 | **Pet Training** | pet advancement + taming marks, charms, hero roulette + shards, intel |
| 4 | **Gear & Troops** | governor charm score, hero gear (forgehammer, mithril, exclusive widgets), troop training T1–T11 |
| 5 | **Combined** | building upgrades, governor gear, speedups, all hero gear, intel, gathering — **full rates**, not reduced; it's the cleanup day |

⚠ Day 4 appears as "Hero Development" on one optimizer tab and "Gear & Troops" in the
optimizer's prose + kingshotmastery; the task list is hero **gear** + troops, no shards.

### Duel of the Elites (new prep layer)

- Runs during prep **Stages 1–4**; the top 150 governors by power in each kingdom,
  split into 10 groups of up to 15 per kingdom.
- Daily group race on **Personal Preparation Points**; group rank 1 earns a
  **Preparation Point Bonus for the kingdom on the NEXT prep phase** (kingdom prep
  points only — not personal/alliance/duel points). Bonuses stack.
- Practical: non-whales ignore it; leadership picks winnable groups rather than
  everyone panic-spending.

### Battle phase (context for the page's "battle weekend" strip)

- Battle day ~10:00–22:00 UTC; the **Castle contest runs a full 5 hours**. A single
  alliance holding **2.5 hours continuously** wins on the spot; otherwise the
  alliance with the **most total hold time** when the 5 hours are up takes it. The
  game runs the full window regardless — if no one lands the continuous hold, it
  plays out even once a side is mathematically uncatchable (alliance correction;
  the community sources said "3 hours / 12:00–18:00" — wrong).
- 4 turrets wear down the defending side + give lethality to occupiers.
- **The window itself (alliance):** during 10:00–22:00 UTC players can **teleport
  between the servers** and troop kills score points **All-Out style** — fun to blow
  off steam, but don't burn too many troops/resources. Check world and alliance chat
  for **rules of engagement agreed between the servers**. Shields are invaluable and
  essential for any offline time in the window.
- Field Triage: 30% base troop recovery, up to 90% with Medical Satchels + Rescue Orders.
- Rankings: 6 categories — Personal Prep, Personal Battle, Alliance Prep, Alliance
  Battle, plus combined cross-kingdom ranks.

## 3 · Strongest Governor — structure

- **7-day individual ranking event**, once per 4-week cycle (monthly), 6 kingdoms
  competing cross-kingdom.
- Daily rank rewards at 00:00 UTC; daily milestones + **Challenge Medals (2/day,
  up to 14)**; kingdom personal ranking (top 1000); cross-kingdom personal ranking
  (top 2000, exclusive avatar frames + nameplates); **kingdom top-6 + 250,000
  personal points** → extra kingdom rewards.
- **Milestones scale with Town Center group**: TC 1–21 / 22–25 / 26–29 / 30+; group 4
  thresholds ≈ 2× group 1. The live display should ask for TC group.
- Day theme names repeat (Skill Up ×2, Combat Training ×2, Hero Development ×2) but
  each instance has a different active-task set — read the live day, don't assume.

## 4 · The live display — data model

One shared engine, two event calendars. Each calendar is an array of days; each day has:

```
{ event, day, theme, tasks: [{ label, pointsPerUnit, unit, icon }],
  milestones: [thresholds], prep: [strings], copy: [the KingShot block lines] }
```

- **Cycle input:** the schedule is global — the page derives today's day from the
  date, anchored at **cycle day 1 = Monday 2026-08-17** (a one-line constant in
  `kvk.js`), with a calibration date input ("day 1 of the cycle falls on…") persisted
  in localStorage for when the device date differs from the game's. Stepper +
  week-jump buttons are for peeking at other days; a "today" button snaps back, and
  the card re-checks the date on focus/visibility so an open tab rolls over cleanly.
- **Today's card:** which event is live (KvK prep day 1–5, SG day 1–7, battle weekend,
  or off-week), the theme, the ✅/🆗/🚫 efficient-spend list for the *current* day, and
  what **not** to touch today (the 🚫 column is the real value).
- **Prep strip:** for the next event(s), the save-list + buff ritual (Wolf, Double Time
  Decree, Chief Minister, intel queue, hospital empty, bison stacking ⚠).
- **Copy button:** builds the KingShot block for today (see §5). One button per scope:
  today's chart, or the full prep-week chart.
- Site plumbing: new `kvk-strongest-governor/` directory (per AGENTS.md mechanics), a
  `data-page` theme block + dust rules in `events.css`, a per-page CSS file, a per-page
  JS registering via `window.BH.registerPage` (the swordland/vikings toy pattern), nav
  updates in topbar + footer of every page, and `i18n/<lang>.js` keys (`kvk.*` / `sg.*`).
  The day tables are data — put them in the page JS (like the swordland constants) and
  keep copy in i18n. Point tables are community-curated, not formulas; MATHS.md is
  untouched unless we derive something (e.g. points-per-min comparisons).

## 5 · The KingShot copy format spec

Reality from the sources:

- **512-character message limit** in KingShot chat — the killer constraint.
- ⚠ **There is also a fixed width and a LINE CAP** (corrected after a real paste):
  the full 17-line chart comes back from KingShot with its rows merged — the
  alliance's paste showed the lines collapsing and concatenating after ~10 lines,
  and blank lines collapsing too. Multi-line blocks must stay **≤6 lines, no blank
  lines**. The chart therefore CANNOT be pasted as one block; the copy generator
  emits **one compact per-day block per message** instead (see below).
- Chat supports **rich text via HTML tags** (color, size, bold); kingshot.net's
  [Text Formatter](https://kingshot.net/text-formatter) generates the HTML and has a
  **"Unicode Bypass"** option that uses Unicode characters to get around Unity's tag
  filtering, with a quirk: the formatted send needs a player tagged in chat to work.
- The "limited unicode set" is therefore Unity's filter, not the font: emoji and
  full-width characters demonstrably survive (the alliance chart uses 👑 ✅ 🆗 🚫 and
  full-width １ ２ ３ ４ ５ and ｜ and they paste fine).
- **Item icons are Private-Use-Area codepoints** that KingShot renders as its item
  emoji: observed mappings from the chart — TrueGold `` (U+E010), "true master" ``
  (U+E017), building `` (U+E013), troop `` (U+E014), research `` (U+E015), hammer
  `` (U+E016). They render in KingShot; on the website they're tofu, so the site
  shows its own labels and only the *copy* may carry the PUA icons. ⚠ Verify the
  remaining rows (hero shard, master, wheel, gathering, intel, pets, gov charm, gov
  gear, widget, mithril) against a KingShot paste before shipping icons; text labels
  are the safe fallback.

### Safe character set (for the copy generator)

Use only: ASCII + `✅ 🆗 🚫` + full-width digits `１ ２ ３ ４ ５ ６ ７` + `｜`
(full-width vertical bar) + `·` + `– —` + the known PUA item icons (optional) +
`👑` for the crown header. Avoid box-drawing (`│ ─ ┌`), arrows, and exotic symbols
unless a test paste proves they render. Plain `|` also works if ｜ is ever an issue.

### The copy block (corrected after a real KingShot paste)

The full chart does **not** survive a paste (fixed width + line cap merge the rows),
so the generator emits **one compact per-day block** — 5 lines, no blank lines,
short chat labels:

```
👑KVK PREP · DAY 2👑
Basic Skills Up
✅ Hero shard · Master · Wheel · Building · Research · Gathering
🆗 Truegold · Tempered TG
🚫 Troop · Intel · Pets · Gov charm · Gov gear · Widget · Mithril · Hammer
```

Every copy scope follows the same rules:
1. **≤6 lines, no blank lines**, short labels (the alliance's chat words: Truegold,
   Tempered TG, Hero shard, Master, Building, Troop, Research, Wheel, Gathering,
   Intel, Pets, Gov charm, Gov gear, Widget, Mithril, Hammer).
2. Prep week = one per-day block per message. SG days compress to header + value
   lines + a gems/intel caution. Battle and save lists condense to 3–4 lines.
3. The character counter stays; the 512 splitter remains as a backstop (a split
   block is sent as separate messages, each under the line cap).
4. Plain text to clipboard — no HTML; the formatter's color markup is a separate
   power-user path.
