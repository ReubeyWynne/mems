1 Every rally is a team shot

A bear rally doesn't have one damage number that gets shared out. It has one number per participant — and that changes everything.

THE LEAD Sets the punch

A rally hits exactly as hard per troop as the lead's stats allow. Attack % and Lethality %, per troop type. Everyone in the rally shares the same leader multiplier — it's the same for all 15 of you.

THE PARTICIPANT Scores on your own troops

Your damage is calculated per player, from your own march — using the square root of your troop count. The rally's total troop count never enters your personal damage.

The lead's stats × your troops, scored per player. That single sentence explains every strategy on this page.

2 The damage formula

One formula per player, per rally, added over your three troop types:

damage = Σ baset × At(lead) × √(nt)

nt = troops of type t in your march · At(lead) = (1 + atk%) × (1 + lethality%) of the lead for type t

Base damage per troop type — the game's constants

Archers4.4 / 3 ≈ 1.47hit hardest per troop — that's the 4 × 1.1 bonus vs infantry

You don't need your own attack stats to contribute: the lead's stats apply to every participant. Your job is showing up with the right troops.

3 The square root: double ≠ double

The √ sits on your troop count. That makes damage grow fast at first, then flatten hard — and it's the entire reason this event plays the way it does.

  • 1× troops → 1.0 damage
  • 2× troops → 1.41 damage — only +41%
  • 4× troops → 2.0 damage
  • 16× troops → 4.0 damage

The first troops hit hardest; every extra troop is worth a little less than the one before it. So the fastest way to more damage is not a bigger march — it's more marches.

4 Split it: the rally and your marches

Because the √ is per march, how many marches carry a pile of troops matters more than how big the pile is. Two rules fall straight out of the formula.

Rule A — fill the rally, don't stuff it

A rally holds 1 lead + 14 joiners = 15 slots. A rally's troop cap (say 900k) divided by its slots is the fair share — about 60k per player.

Here's the kicker: rally damage with a fixed troop budget grows with the number of joiners, because j joiners × √(troops/j) = √(j × troops).

Every slot you leave empty, or every extra march you stuff in, shrinks j — and with it the whole rally's damage. Stuffing 200k into a 15-slot rally squeezes out ~3 fair-share players and the room loses more than you gain.

Rule B — spread your marches across rallies

Your pool P across Q simultaneous marches: one march scores √P, but Q marches score √(Q × P) — same troops, Q ≈ √Q times the damage. You can field up to 6 marches at once.

Try it — your troops, your marches

100,000 troops per march · 2.45× the damage of one big march — nothing extra spent.

One big march leaves damage on the table. Use every march slot, every wave, across different rallies — that's the whole game.

5 Keep it moving

A rally fires exactly 5:00 after launch. Troops sitting in a queue are troops that aren't scoring — the event only runs 30 minutes.

Staggered waves turn one giant fire at 5:00 into a fire roughly every minute:

launch 0:00 · fire 5:00
Wave 2
launch 1:00 · fire 6:00
Wave 3
launch 2:00 · fire 7:00
Wave 4
launch 3:00 · fire 8:00
0:002:004:006:008:00
  • Fire = launch + 5:00, always.
  • A march parked in a rally that already fired is missing the next one.
  • Every missed fire is damage you're not earning — keep marches moving.

6 Send what the leader likes

The leader's stats multiply each troop type separately — and the types aren't equal. Archers hit hardest per troop, so a leader's ideal mix is usually heavy on archers. Match it and your damage jumps.

Lean archers

Archers ≈ 1.47 base vs cavalry 1.0 and infantry 0.33. Same leader, same march size — archers simply hit harder per troop.

Keep ~5k of a type or skip it

Below about 5,000 troops of a type, the √ barely registers — it's noise. Better to top up the types the leader's ratio wants.

If you like the maths

The ideal split of your march follows the squared ratio of (base × leader stat) per type — that's what a rally lead's "preferred mix" is.

7 Why splitting grows the whole alliance

Damage crosses reward thresholds — roughly 47M, 90M, 175M on the relative scale — for Forgehammers. Because damage is scored per player, spreading troops so everyone crosses a bracket grows the alliance faster than one whale hogging every rally.

Rewards follow your own damage

Your bracket is set by your total — not the rally's. A stuffed rally mostly helps the few who fit inside it.

Everyone crossing beats one whale

Fair-share rallies mean more players in higher brackets — more Forgehammers for the alliance, every single run.

8 The bottom line: four rules

  1. 1

    Split your marches

    Q rallies ≈ √Q × the damage of one big march. Never one giant march — spread across different rallies.

  2. 2

    Fill every rally

    14 joiners at fair share ≈ √14 × the damage of one player carrying the same troops. Leave no slot empty, stuff no rally.

  3. 3

    Match the lead's mix

    Lean archers, keep ~5k floors, send the ratio the lead wants.

  4. 4

    Keep moving

    Fire = launch + 5:00. Staggered waves = a fire every minute. Parked troops score zero.
