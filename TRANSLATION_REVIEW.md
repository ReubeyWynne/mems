## 3. Cross-language patterns

1. **The cap gap (all 16)** — see §1. Highest-priority fix everywhere.
2. **Gender of the rally lead (zh-Hans, zh-Hant)** — `model.card1.title` "Sets the power"
   rendered 「威力由他定」, which genders an unknown lead male. Fix: 「威力由队长定/隊長定」.
3. **Named-hero gender errors** — pt-BR `heroes.j.yeonwoo.why` refers to Chenko (female, EN
   "her") as male (`com o dele`); de/it drop or mask Ava's gender (`Anführer`/`un leader`).
4. **`heroes.l.weewoo.why` subject ambiguity (tr, ko)** — EN "…but her widget still wins the
   arithmetic" is ambiguous in the source itself (whose widget — Weewoo's or Yang's?); Turkish
   commits to Yang's, Korean to Yang's too but flags it. Needs author confirmation, then align.
5. **HTML-fidelity violation (pt-BR only)** — `heroes.d1` dropped the `<b>` tags around
   "joiners"/"leaders" (`rally <b>joiners</b> and rally <b>leaders</b>`). All other files are
   byte-identical on tags/classes/ids.
6. **Voice-rule nits** — several files use default-masculine forms for the reader where the
   grammar allows neutrality: fr (`pas passé`, `tout seul`), it (`visto`/`fatto`),
   de (generic-masculine 3rd person ×4), es (`el que se une`), pl (`powinieneś` — accepted
   convention), ko (`내 병력` first-person slip in `meta.description`).
7. **Register wobble in easter eggs** — vi mixes `tao/mày` with `bạn`; ja uses `お前` in
   `heroes.out` (normally reserved for the bear's voice); th transliterates slang freely
   (acceptable). Unify per egg section.
8. **Terminology drift** — id: English troop terms in the mix section vs Indonesian in the
   heroes section; tr: translated subscripts in two math keys (`f_piy` etc.) vs untouched
   single-letter variables elsewhere; ko: `참가자` once vs `합류자` everywhere else; fr:
   `rallys` once vs `rallies`; zh: `S-tier`/`S級`/`S` mixed.
9. **Minor meaning drifts worth fixing** — ar `mix.card2.body` "a dozen" → «عشرات» (dozens);
   it `mix.note` drops "bear"; it `hero.lede` drops "required"; zh-Hant `timing.d6` adds a
   "will make it in time" claim; zh-Hant `foot.credit` "tested" → "survives scrutiny";
   pl `egg.whisper4` softens "greeding"; ja `heroes.joiners.lede` "get cards" → "obtain cards";
   th `egg.gossip3` slogan → statement; es `marches.d` "pool" → "fondo".

## 5. What's in great shape

- **Math/number fidelity is essentially perfect across all 16 files** — every `√ × ÷ ≈ Σ ∝ ≤ → ⅓`,
  formula, subscript, time (`5:00`, `30:00`, `0:00–8:00`), range, percentage and the `{n}`
  placeholder survives untouched (verified per-key programmatically by several agents).
- **HTML is byte-identical in 15 of 16 files** (pt-BR's `heroes.d1` is the sole violation).
- **All proper nouns preserved** — Bear Hunt, Forgehammer, Frakinator, MadNess, Kingshot, every
  hero/player name (incl. r3lax, xglitchx, lucy, shadow, Spooks), Sundering Wounds, KvK.
- **RTL handling in `ar` passes** — progression arrows flipped → to ←, formulas wrapped in
  `<span dir="ltr">`, zero Arabic-Indic digits.
- Several dictionaries are genuinely excellent prose: **ru, ar, zh-Hans, de** were called
  near-native by their reviewers; easter-egg voices survive well in pl, es, ko, ja.
